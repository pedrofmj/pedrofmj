# Platform Documentation Template

Use this structure when adding a new platform to the portfolio.

Before publishing a new page, replace the guidance under each section with platform-specific public documentation and remove anything that is not applicable.

---

## Platform Status

- Status: active, maintained, archived, concept, or research-oriented
- Documentation level: public summary, architecture summary, research mapping, or complete public case study
- Public boundary: state what is intentionally excluded from the page

---

## Purpose

Explain what the platform does and who it serves.

---

## Motivation

Explain why the platform exists and what problem made it necessary.

---

## Business Problem

Describe the real-world problem without exposing customer data, private processes, credentials, or proprietary implementation details.

---

## Architecture Overview

Describe the platform through public-safe boundaries, modules, workflows, and responsibilities.

Include a sanitized Mermaid diagram when possible.

---

## Technologies

List technology categories and major public frameworks only.

Avoid private infrastructure names, internal hosts, credentials, or repository links.

---

## Engineering Decisions

Document the major design decisions and tradeoffs.

---

## Usage Scenario

Describe one realistic public-safe scenario that shows how the platform is used.

---

## Technical Challenge

Explain one important technical challenge and how the architecture addresses it.

---

## Engineering Lesson Learned

Capture one reusable lesson from building or evolving the platform.

---

## Platform Relationships

List relationships with other platforms in this repository.

---

## Screenshots

Reference sanitized screenshots or diagrams only.

---

## Future Roadmap

Organize roadmap items by near-term, medium-term, and long-term work.

Label each item as engineering quality, product evolution, or research.

---

## Related Research

Link to related research directions, proposals, reports, experiments, or datasets.

---

## Research Opportunities

List possible future publications, experiments, technical reports, and datasets.
